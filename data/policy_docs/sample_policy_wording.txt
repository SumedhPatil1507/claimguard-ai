SECTION 1 — POLICY COVERAGE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.1 Own Damage (Motor)
The insurer agrees to indemnify the policyholder against loss or damage to the
insured vehicle arising from accidental collision, fire, explosion, self-ignition,
lightning, theft, burglary, housebreaking, riot, strike, malicious act, flood,
cyclone, storm, tempest, inundation, hailstorm, frost, landslide, rockslide, or
during transit by rail, road, inland waterway, lift, elevator, or air.

1.2 Third-Party Liability (Motor)
The insurer shall indemnify the policyholder against any liability incurred in
respect of death or bodily injury to a third party or damage to third-party
property arising out of the use of the insured vehicle on public roads, as
mandated under the Motor Vehicles Act. The limit of indemnity for property damage
is capped at INR 7,50,000 per accident unless an enhanced limit rider is endorsed.

1.3 Personal Accident Cover
A lump-sum benefit of INR 15,00,000 is payable to the named policyholder in the
event of accidental death or permanent total disablement sustained while driving,
mounting, or dismounting from the insured vehicle. Optional covers for unnamed
passengers may be added by endorsement.

1.4 Health Insurance Benefits
Under the health floater option, the insurer reimburses or pays on a cashless
basis the expenses of hospitalisation exceeding 24 consecutive hours for illness
or accidental injury. Covered expenses include room and boarding (subject to
room-rent limits as per sum insured slab), surgeon and anaesthetist fees,
diagnostic tests, pharmacy expenses up to the sub-limit, and post-hospitalisation
expenses for a period of 60 days after discharge.

1.5 Day-Care Procedures
Medical procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalisation but are performed
under general or local anaesthesia in a registered day-care centre are covered,
subject to the list of approved procedures maintained by the insurer and updated
annually.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 — EXCLUSIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Motor Exclusions
The insurer shall not be liable for any loss or damage arising while the insured
vehicle is driven by a person without a valid driving licence, or while the driver
is under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any psychoactive substance. Damage
attributable to mechanical or electrical breakdown, gradual wear and tear, tyre
burst unless accompanied by damage to the vehicle, and depreciation is not covered.
Loss or damage arising out of war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities,
civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, or nuclear perils is excluded.

2.2 Health Exclusions
Expenses arising from pre-existing conditions are excluded for the first 36 months
of continuous coverage unless specifically waived by endorsement after medical
underwriting. Costs related to cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (unless arising
from accidental injury), corrective eyewear, infertility treatment, experimental
therapies, and self-inflicted injuries are excluded. Hospitalisation primarily for
diagnostic purposes without active treatment is not covered.

SECTION 3 — CLAIMS PROCEDURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1 Notification
The policyholder must notify the insurer of any loss, damage, accident, or illness
requiring a claim within 7 calendar days of the event. For theft, an FIR must be
lodged with the nearest police station and a copy submitted along with the claim
intimation.

3.2 Documents Required
Motor claim: duly completed claim form, copy of registration certificate, driving
licence, insurance certificate, fitness certificate (commercial vehicles), FIR
(theft or third-party cases), survey/repair estimate, original bills and receipts.
Health claim: completed claim form, original hospital discharge summary, itemised
hospital bills, all diagnostic reports, prescriptions, and pharmacy receipts,
treating doctor's certificate confirming diagnosis and necessity of hospitalisation.

3.3 Repair Authorisation
For motor own-damage claims, the policyholder must obtain prior authorisation from
the insurer's surveyor or the designated garage network before commencing repairs,
except in cases where emergency repairs are necessary to ensure vehicle safety.
Repairs at non-network garages may be subject to a higher depreciation schedule
and require an independent surveyor's report at the policyholder's request.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 — WAITING PERIODS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1 General Waiting Period
All health benefits are subject to a general waiting period of 30 days from the
inception date of the policy. This waiting period does not apply to claims arising
purely from accidents.

4.2 Specific Disease Waiting Period
Conditions including but not limited to cataract, hernia, joint replacement, and
specified degenerative disorders are subject to a 2-year waiting period. The
complete list is specified in Annexure A of the policy schedule.

4.3 Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period
Pre-existing diseases as declared in the proposal form are covered after 36 months
of continuous and uninterrupted renewal without a break in cover exceeding 30 days.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 — RENEWAL TERMS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The policy shall be renewed annually prior to the expiry date. A grace period of
30 days is permitted for renewal without loss of continuity benefits; however, no
claims for events occurring during the lapse period will be entertained. Premium
revision, if any, shall be intimated at least 60 days before the renewal date.
Policyholders who have been claim-free for three consecutive years are entitled to
a No-Claim Bonus as specified in the premium schedule. The insurer reserves the
right to decline renewal only on the grounds of confirmed fraud or material
misrepresentation, subject to IRDAI guidelines.
