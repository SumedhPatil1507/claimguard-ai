IRDAI REGULATORY GUIDELINES — SUMMARY FOR CLAIMGUARD AI TRAINING
DEMO / TRAINING SUMMARY ONLY — NOT LEGAL ADVICE

Source reference: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)
Compiled for: ClaimGuard AI Platform (internal training corpus)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. CLAIM SETTLEMENT TIMELINES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Under IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, insurers are
required to settle all claims within 30 days of receipt of the last necessary
document. Where the claim requires further investigation, the insurer must complete
the investigation and settle the claim within 45 days of receiving the claim
intimation. Any delay beyond these timelines attracts interest on the claim amount
at 2% above the bank rate. Repudiation of claims must be communicated in writing
with a detailed rationale citing the specific policy clause and regulation.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every insurer must have a Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) and acknowledge
policyholder grievances within 3 working days. Final resolution must be communicated
within 15 days of receipt. Policyholders dissatisfied with the insurer's resolution
may escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman within their jurisdiction. IRDAI's Bima
Bharosa portal serves as the centralised grievance registration mechanism. Insurers
must maintain and submit monthly grievance data to IRDAI in the prescribed format.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. KYC AND AML REQUIREMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

IRDAI mandates Know Your Customer (KYC) verification for all new policies where
the annual premium exceeds INR 1,00,000 or the sum insured exceeds INR 10,00,000.
Acceptable KYC documents include Aadhaar-linked verification, PAN card, Voter ID,
or Passport. Insurers must conduct Customer Due Diligence (CDD) at onboarding and
Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-value or politically exposed persons.
Under Anti-Money Laundering (AML) obligations, insurers must file Suspicious
Activity Reports (SAR) with the Financial Intelligence Unit — India (FIU-IND) for
transactions exhibiting red flags, including structuring of premiums, unusual
early-stage large claims, and frequent policy surrender for cash benefits.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. PORTABILITY RIGHTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Policyholders holding individual health insurance policies have the right to
transfer their policy from one insurer to another while retaining credit for
waiting periods already served. The portability request must be submitted at least
45 days before the policy renewal date. The receiving insurer may underwrite the
policy but cannot impose fresh waiting periods for conditions for which the
policyholder has already completed the waiting period with the previous insurer.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. FREE-LOOK PERIOD
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Policyholders purchasing a new policy have the right to review the terms and
conditions and return the policy within 15 days of receipt (30 days for policies
sold through distance marketing channels, including online and tele-sales). If the
policyholder exercises the free-look option, the insurer must refund the premium
paid after deducting a proportionate risk premium for the coverage period and any
medical examination costs incurred.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. FRAUD REPORTING OBLIGATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

IRDAI's Insurance Fraud Monitoring Framework requires insurers to establish a
dedicated fraud management unit, maintain a fraud repository, and report detected
fraud cases to the IRDAI fraud reporting portal. Insurers must share fraud data
with the Insurance Information Bureau of India (IIB) to facilitate industry-wide
fraud intelligence. Collusive fraud involving agents, intermediaries, or repair
networks must be reported within 7 working days of detection.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. DATA LOCALISATION AND PRIVACY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

IRDAI guidelines require that all policyholder data, including personal and health
information, be stored on servers located within India. Cross-border data transfer
is permitted only with explicit policyholder consent and must comply with applicable
data protection legislation. Insurers must implement data encryption at rest and in
transit, and conduct annual information security audits.
